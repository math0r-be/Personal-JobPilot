# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Solo job seekers (developers, marketers, sales professionals) actively managing their job search. They track multiple applications, tailor CVs for each role, and need a clear overview of where they stand. Context: at their computer, alone, in a focused workflow. They are competent professionals who value their time.

## Product Purpose

JobPilot is a 100% local, open-source job search command center. It helps job seekers organize applications, manage CVs and cover letters with AI assistance, track pipeline status, and land the right role faster — all without sending data to third parties. Success means reducing the friction of a job search so the user can focus on what matters: getting hired.

## Brand Personality

Serious, professional, sober. Competent and trustworthy without being flashy. This is a tool for people who take their career seriously; it respects their intelligence and their time.

## Anti-references

No startup bling-bling — no neon gradients, no gamification badges, no candy-pop colors, no trendy animations that serve no purpose. Not SaaS-corporate either — no HubSpot/Salesforce clone, no heavy admin template feel. Nothing that screams "I'm a startup."

## Design Principles

1. **Productivity first** — the interface should never slow the user down. Every click, every animation, every layout decision must justify itself in terms of speed and clarity.
2. **Clear and predictable** — no surprises, no dead ends, no mystery meat. The user should always know where they are, what just happened, and what to do next.
3. **Quiet confidence** — the design breathes competence without overdoing it. Strong hierarchy, restrained color, deliberate spacing. The tool feels solid because it doesn't need to show off.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Support for reduced motion. Color combinations must be perceivable by users with common forms of color blindness (deuteranopia, protanopia).
